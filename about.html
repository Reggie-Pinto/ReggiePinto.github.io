Our Story

Raising Persians with Heart

Only Persians began over 7 years ago when we fell in love with the gentle, affectionate nature of Persian cats. What started as a personal passion quickly grew into a dedicated breeding program focused on health, temperament, and beauty.

Every kitten born at Only Persians is raised in our family home — not a cattery. They are handled daily, socialized with children and other pets, and given the love and attention they need to thrive.

We are committed to breeding Persian cats that are not only beautiful but also healthy, well-tempered, and ready to be cherished family members.

  • TICA and CFA registered breeding program
  • All breeding cats health-tested
  • Kittens raised in family home environment
  • Lifetime support for all our families
  • Transparent and ethical breeding practices
