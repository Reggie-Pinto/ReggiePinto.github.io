About Us

A Home Built on Love & Dedication

At Only Persians, every kitten is raised in our family home — not a cattery. We believe cats thrive with human interaction, warmth, and care from day one.

All our kittens are vet-checked, vaccinated, dewormed, and microchipped before going to their forever homes.

  • Vet health certificates provided
  • Vaccinated & dewormed
  • Lifetime breeder support
  • Pedigree documentation available
